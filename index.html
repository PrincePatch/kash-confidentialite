Kash — Legal & support

Android app com.florian.budgettickets, published by the developer. Last updated: 9 September 2026.

Kash is a household budget app built around scanning till receipts. You photograph a receipt, the app reads it on the phone itself, and the spending is filed into your categories and envelope budgets. No account, no bank connection, no server: everything stays on your device, in a database encrypted with SQLCipher, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.
