1.What the app is

Kash is an Android app for keeping track of a household budget by scanning till receipts. You photograph a receipt or type it in by hand; the app reads it on the device, splits it into item lines, files it into categories — including the ones you create — and compares it with your envelope budgets, with an end-of-month projection.

Kash also detects recurring payments as subscriptions, keeps a price history for each item, lets you set up a local household with its members and a split key, and can produce a CSV export, a monthly PDF report and an encrypted local backup archive. An optional biometric lock protects access to the app.

Kash works offline, without an account and without a server. Installing and using the app means accepting these terms. If you do not accept them, do not use the app.

4.Accuracy of automatic reading

Kash asks you to check every receipt before saving it and flags the fields read with low confidence. That check is yours to make.

Totals, statistics and end-of-month projections are indicative and depend entirely on what has been entered.

Kash is not an accounting tool. It produces no accounting, tax or legally binding document. The CSV export and the PDF report are convenience documents for your own use: they are neither invoices nor certificates and prove nothing by themselves.

Kash erkennt außerdem wiederkehrende Zahlungen als Abos, führt einen Preisverlauf je Artikel, erlaubt einen lokalen Haushalt mit Mitgliedern und Verteilungsschlüssel und kann einen CSV-Export, einen monatlichen PDF-Bericht sowie ein verschlüsseltes lokales Sicherungsarchiv erzeugen. Eine optionale biometrische Sperre schützt den Zugang zur App.

Kash arbeitet offline, ohne Konto und ohne Server. Mit der Installation und Nutzung der App erkennst du diese Bedingungen an. Wenn du sie nicht annimmst, nutze die App nicht.
