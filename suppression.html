Deleting your data

Kash app com.florian.budgettickets, published by the developer. Last updated: 9 September 2026.

No request is needed. Kash has no account and no server, so the developer holds none of your data.

Everything is on your phone, and you erase it yourself in a few seconds — either from inside the app, or by uninstalling it. There is nothing to ask for, and nobody else could do it for you.

1.Erase everything from inside the app

If you want to keep a copy of anything, do that first: More › Export produces a CSV file and a monthly PDF report, and More › Backup produces a complete encrypted archive including the photos. After deletion, nothing can be recovered.

  1. Open Kash.
  2. In the bottom bar, tap More.
  3. Under Your data, tap Data & privacy.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap Delete all my data.
  5. First confirmation — Delete all data? — tap Continue.
  6. Second confirmation — Last confirmation — tap Delete everything.
  7. The app replies All your data has been deleted. It is done — and it cannot be undone.

2.Or simply uninstall the app

Press and hold the Kash icon, then choose Uninstall. Or go to Android Settings › Apps › Kash › Uninstall.

Uninstalling removes the app’s private storage in one go: the encrypted database, the receipt photos, the settings and the encryption key. Nothing is left behind on the phone, and nothing was ever copied to Google Drive, since Android’s automatic backup is disabled for this app.

4.Nothing is retained, and there is no waiting period

Kash has no account and no server, so the developer holds none of your data: there is nothing to keep after deletion, no retention period, and nothing that could be restored.
